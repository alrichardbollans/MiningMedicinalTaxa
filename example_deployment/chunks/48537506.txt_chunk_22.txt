It is often used for the treatment of upper respiratory infections, especially tonsillitis and other causes of sore throat; by oral ingestion. It is sometimes also applied topically to treat boils, carbuncles, cellulitis and other skin infections. A woman applied "Lushenwan" to her neck to relieve neck pain. Within two days, she 211 developed an itchy erythematous rash on her neck. The erythematous patches bear well-defined margins, lichenification and excoriation. By the next morning, her eyelids had become red, swollen and very itchy. On examination, both eyelids were red, swollen and edematous. The borders of the lesions were well-defined and excoriations were seen. Patch tests confirmed that "LiushenwarT caused an irritant rather than an allergic type of reactions.
